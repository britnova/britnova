## Challenge
Veridia Logistics was processing thousands of international shipping manifest documents manually every single day. The manual transcription was error-prone, bottlenecked supply chain dispatch schedules, and caused significant customs delays due to minor clerical errors.

## Solution
We engineered a custom AI document processing platform that integrated optical character recognition (OCR) and localized large language models (LLMs). The platform reads multi-format PDFs, extracts complex tabular shipment lists, cross-references them with customs databases, and flags anomalies directly inside a React dashboard.

## Results
The system went from proof of concept to full production in under 8 weeks. It now processes over 95% of manifests without human intervention, ensuring rapid custom clearance times.
